Sky Exchange

When I was small I heard about a game where people write messages to strangers and then tie them to balloons and let them go.

I took a picture of the sky over Japan, attached it to a balloon and let it go in California. Then I released a picture of the sky in California back in Japan.

Will the two skies meet? Where will the balloon end up?
